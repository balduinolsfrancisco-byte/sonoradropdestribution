# SonoraDrop Distribution

SonoraDrop is an artist-centric digital music distribution, rights management, and automated royalty split platform built natively for Android using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

## Key Features

- **Global Music Distribution**: Distribute singles, EPs, and full albums to over 150+ digital streaming platforms and social networks (Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok, YouTube Music, Amazon Music, Tidal, Deezer, Pandora, and Meta/Instagram).
- **Automated Royalty Split Pay**: Configure collaborator percentages per release (producers, featured vocalists, songwriters, engineers) with automated split payouts.
- **Direct Payouts & Statements**: Manage available earnings, view monthly statements, and request withdrawals via PayPal, Bank Wire / PIX, or Stripe.
- **Cover Song Mechanical Licensing**: Automated mechanical clearance calculation and statutory compliance handling for cover song releases.
- **Real-Time Stream Telemetry**: Visual streams velocity curves, listener demographics, territorial rankings, and platform market share analytics.
- **Smart Pre-Save & Share Links**: Generate dedicated landing links (`sonora.drop.link/...`) with one-tap clipboard sharing.
- **Release Lifecycle Management**: Comprehensive metadata management (ISRC, UPC barcodes, explicit lyrics flags, tracklist sequencing, delivery tracking).
- **Verified Artist Profiles**: Artist discography, bio management, and verified badge credentials.

## Technology Stack

- **Platform**: Android SDK 36 (Java 21)
- **UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose with Material Design 3 (M3)
- **Architecture**: MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel) with StateFlow
- **Theme**: Dark electric sonic theme with neon accents
- **Design & Layout**: Responsive layout with full Edge-to-Edge window insets and adaptive icon support
